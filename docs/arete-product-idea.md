# Arete Product Idea

**Name:** Arete  
**Pronunciation:** `ah-REH-tay`  
**Origin:** Greek *aretē* — excellence, capability, and the achievement of one’s potential.  
**Tagline:** **Make today count.**

## One-line pitch

**Arete is a sponsor-funded daily skill challenge inside Nimiq Pay that gives users a reason to return, learn, and earn visible progress—without paid entry, chance, or pooled player money.**

## The product idea

Daily games build habits because they are short, social, and easy to return to. Wallets struggle with the opposite problem: users create one, then have few practical reasons to open it again.

Arete is the daily practice layer for Nimiq Pay. A player completes one short, deterministic challenge, sees an immediate score and rank, protects a streak, earns reputation, and discovers a useful Nimiq merchant or ecosystem action. Sponsors fund campaign rewards as transparent marketing spend, never through player deposits.

> **One small challenge. A stronger wallet habit.**

## The right MVP

The first experience is a five-question, deterministic Nimiq ecosystem/sponsor quiz with a speed tie-breaker.

- Accuracy ranks first; completion time only resolves a tie.
- One ranked attempt per wallet per day.
- Rules appear before play; correct answers appear once the challenge closes.
- The daily challenge takes under two minutes.
- Rewards are sponsor-funded, eligibility-based, and may be reviewed manually in the MVP.

Do not launch as a collection of mini-games. A perfect daily quiz loop is more valuable than five half-built game types.

## Core loop

1. Open Arete inside Nimiq Pay.
2. See today’s challenge, rules, sponsor context, and any reward eligibility.
3. Complete the challenge.
4. Receive score, rank, streak and badge progress.
5. Discover one relevant merchant/payment/ecosystem action.
6. Share the daily result card.
7. Return tomorrow to continue the practice.

## Audience

| Priority | Audience | Job |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Nimiq Pay users and crypto-curious community members | Build a practical daily wallet habit through a fast challenge. |
| 2 | Nimiq merchants and ecosystem projects | Earn qualified attention through a transparent sponsored challenge. |
| 3 | Contest judges and testers | Experience obvious Nimiq utility in a polished Mini App. |

## Sponsor model

Sponsors fund campaigns as marketing spend. They receive visible campaign placement and later, privacy-conscious aggregate engagement metrics. They do **not** buy leaderboard outcomes, see wallet activity without consent, or gain permission to make the app feel like advertising.

A sponsor card must answer:

- Who funds the reward?
- What does the player gain?
- What action or discovery does this campaign invite?
- What rules/eligibility apply?

## Contest-safe product boundaries

Arete is **not** gambling, betting, a sweepstake, or a prize-pool product.

- No player entry fee.
- No pooled player funds.
- No house take.
- No chance-based winner selection.
- No deceptive reward claims.
- Sponsor-funded rewards only.
- Published rules, deterministic scoring, anti-fraud checks and visible reward status.

## Why Nimiq Pay

Nimiq Pay supplies the wallet context that turns a daily challenge into a portable, wallet-linked practice record. NIM should be used meaningfully for sponsor funding and, only after eligibility/review, reward distribution. This creates a legitimate NIM incentive without making users pay to play.

## Long-term potential

Arete can grow from one daily quiz into an ecosystem engagement layer:

- merchant discovery challenges;
- safe payment and wallet-learning modules;
- sponsored educational campaigns;
- reputation and achievement history;
- opt-in merchant action verification;
- community leagues after the core loop is proven.
