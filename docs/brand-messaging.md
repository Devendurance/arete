# Arete Brand Messaging

## Brand core

**Arete** — Greek *aretē*: excellence, capability, and the achievement of one’s potential.  
Pronunciation: **ah-REH-tay**.

> **Make today count.**

### Essence

> **Excellence leaves a record.**

### Product principle

> **Earn the return visit.**

## Positioning

**Category:** Sponsor-funded daily skill challenge Mini App for Nimiq Pay.

> For Nimiq Pay users who need a practical reason to return to their wallet, Arete is a daily skill challenge that turns a short, deterministic practice into wallet-linked streaks, reputation, and useful ecosystem discovery. Unlike paid-entry crypto games or generic quiz apps, Arete creates repeat engagement without player deposits, chance, or prize pools.

## Message hierarchy

| Layer | Message |
|---|---|
| Hero | **Make today count.** |
| Descriptor | One short daily skill challenge for Nimiq Pay. |
| Benefit | Build a stronger wallet habit, one useful practice at a time. |
| Proof | Skill-only rules, server-verified score, visible streak, sponsor-funded rewards. |
| CTA | Start today’s challenge. |

## Messaging pillars

| Pillar | Message | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| A reason to return | One quick daily practice gives the wallet a repeat purpose. | Under-two-minute challenge and a growing personal archive. |
| Progress you can see | Every completed challenge creates an Earned Record. | Date, score, time, rank and wallet-linked history. |
| Skill before luck | Players are ranked by published, deterministic rules. | Accuracy-first scoring, server validation, anti-fraud review. |
| Sponsorship without noise | Campaigns support useful practice rather than interrupt it. | Visible sponsor context, eligibility rules and no paid entry. |
| Nimiq-native by design | Wallet context makes progress portable; NIM supports real campaign/reward activity. | Nimiq Pay Mini App, NIM sponsor funding and eligible reward payout. |

## Voice

| Rule | Use | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Clear and encouraging | “You’re one practice closer to a 3-day streak.” | “Level up your crypto grind.” |
| Transparent | “Reward pending eligibility review.” | “You’ve won—claim now!” |
| Practical | “Today takes about 2 minutes.” | “Unlock limitless learning.” |
| Adult, not academic | “Practice the useful stuff.” | “Gamified blockchain knowledge protocol.” |

### Vocabulary

Use: **practice, record, earned, complete, skill, archive, verified, eligible, precise.**

Avoid: **bet, wager, jackpot, prize pool, earn big, moon, alpha, grind, win money.**

## Claim boundaries

- No paid entry, pooled player funds, chance-based winner selection or house take.
- Sponsor rewards are not guaranteed before eligibility/anti-fraud review.
- NIM is meaningful infrastructure, not a cosmetic badge.
- Sponsors fund campaigns; they do not buy outcomes or player data without consent.

## Final marketing story

> Wallets are easy to open once. The difficult part is giving people a good reason to return. Arete makes that reason small, useful, and repeatable: one daily practice, one visible mark of progress, and a little more confidence using Nimiq Pay.
